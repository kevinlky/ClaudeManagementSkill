# Job Description Writer

## Core Structure

Every JD follows this framework:

- **Position Title** — inclusive, searchable language
- **Department & Reporting Line** — organizational context
- **Position Summary** — 2-3 sentences on role purpose
- **Key Responsibilities** — 5-8 duties using ACTION-IMPACT format
- **Required Qualifications** — only non-negotiable requirements
- **Preferred Qualifications** — open doors for diverse candidates
- **Compensation & Benefits** — transparent salary bands, benefits overview
- **DEI Statement** — commitment to inclusion
- **Application Instructions** — accessibility accommodations offered

## Responsibilities: ACTION-IMPACT Format

Each responsibility starts with an action verb and ties to a business outcome:

- **Pattern**: `[Action verb] + [what you'll do] + [measurable impact]`
- **Example**: "Lead cross-functional product team to deliver 3 major releases annually, impacting 50,000+ users"
- Quantify where possible (X% improvement, $X revenue, X users)
- Show growth potential and learning opportunities

## Qualifications Strategy

- **Required**: Only absolute deal-breakers (certifications, licenses, clearances)
- **Preferred**: Use generously for nice-to-haves
- **Equivalent Experience**: Always include "or X years of related experience"
- **Skills Over Credentials**: Emphasize transferable skills; remove unnecessary degree requirements

## Compensation Transparency

Include when possible (verify local legal requirements):

- Base salary range — e.g., "$95,000 - $145,000 based on experience and location"
- Equity information (if applicable)
- Benefits highlights (healthcare, PTO, retirement, remote options)

## DEI & Belonging Statements

Include one or more:

- "We are an Equal Opportunity Employer"
- "We actively encourage applications from people of color, women, LGBTQ+ individuals, people with disabilities, and other underrepresented groups"
- "Accommodations available for candidates with disabilities"
- "We consider experience outside traditional pathways"

## Workflow

### Step 1: Gather Role Information
Interview hiring manager about: core mission, key success metrics, team structure, biggest challenges, growth opportunities, skills gaps, budget/timeline.

### Step 2: Structure Responsibilities
List 5-8 primary responsibilities, rank by importance, convert to ACTION-IMPACT statements, add success metrics, group related duties.

### Step 3: Define Qualifications
Separate true requirements from nice-to-haves. Include equivalent experience language. Highlight transferable skills. Consider diverse pathways to the role.

### Step 4: Build Inclusive Elements
Apply inclusive language (avoid gendered, ageist, or exclusionary terms). Add DEI statement. Include accessibility accommodations. Verify compensation transparency compliance.

### Step 5: Finalize & Review
Read aloud for tone and clarity. Ensure action verbs throughout. Get manager approval before posting.

### Step 6: Validation Checkpoint
Before publishing, verify:

- [ ] All responsibilities start with action verbs
- [ ] Compensation transparency included
- [ ] DEI statement present and authentic
- [ ] No discriminatory or exclusionary language
- [ ] Reading level appropriate (8th grade or lower)
- [ ] Accessibility accommodations mentioned
- [ ] "Or equivalent experience" included for credentials
- [ ] Manager has reviewed and approved

## Best Practices

1. **Specificity**: Replace vague language with concrete examples
   - Instead of: "Strong communication skills"
   - Use: "Present product strategy to executive team and external partners monthly"

2. **Inclusive Defaults**: Include "or equivalent experience" for all credentials. Accept certifications from international bodies.

3. **Compliance**: Research salary transparency laws by location. Verify non-discrimination language. Check industry-specific requirements.

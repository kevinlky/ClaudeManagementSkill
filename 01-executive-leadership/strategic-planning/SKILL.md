# Strategic Planning

## Phase 1: Strategic Foundation

- Define or refine mission, vision (3-5 year future state), and core values
- Identify 2-4 strategic pillars as key areas of focus

**Checkpoint:** Before proceeding, verify: mission and vision are distinct and specific to the organization, strategic pillars are mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive, and each pillar maps to a measurable business outcome.

## Phase 2: Environmental Analysis

- Conduct SWOT analysis for the organization's specific context
- Map competitive landscape: top 5 competitors' positioning, differentiation, and trajectory
- Identify market gaps and emerging trends relevant to the strategic pillars

**Checkpoint:** Before proceeding, verify: SWOT identifies at least 3 actionable items per quadrant, competitive analysis covers top 5 competitors with specific data points, and at least 2 market opportunities are quantified (TAM, growth rate).

## Phase 3: Strategic Objective Setting

- Define 3-5 strategic objectives for the next 3-5 years
- For each objective, identify key initiatives, milestones, owners, and success metrics
- Write OKRs for the upcoming quarter/year

**Example OKR Set:**

Objective: Establish market leadership in enterprise segment
- KR1: Achieve 60% market share in target verticals
- KR2: Reach $50M ARR from enterprise customers
- KR3: Maintain 95%+ customer satisfaction (NPS >50)
- Initiative: Launch dedicated enterprise sales team
- Initiative: Build enterprise-grade security and compliance features

Write 4-5 organizational OKRs per cycle. Cascade to team levels selectively. Target 60-70% achievement rate on KRs -- if hitting 100%, they are not ambitious enough.

**Checkpoint:** Before proceeding, verify: each objective has 3-4 measurable KRs, every KR has an owner and review cadence, and OKRs are distinct from operational KPIs.

## Phase 4: Strategic Roadmap

Structure the plan across time horizons:

- **Year 1:** Foundation and quick wins -- immediate capabilities, early traction metrics, key hires, revenue targets
- **Year 2-3:** Scaling -- expand proven models, enter new segments, build partnerships
- **Year 4-5:** Market leadership -- dominant position targets, platform/ecosystem plays, adjacent expansion

Apply competitive frameworks (Porter's Five Forces, Blue Ocean, etc.) as analytical tools where relevant to pressure-test the roadmap -- do not enumerate them mechanically.

**Checkpoint:** Before proceeding, verify: Year 1 milestones are specific and time-bound, each horizon has clear success criteria, and resource requirements are estimated for Year 1 initiatives.

## Phase 5: Communication & Alignment

1. Draft strategy document (see `references/templates.md` for full template and OKR checklist)
2. Present to executive team for debate and refinement
3. Cascade to department heads via facilitated sessions
4. Create one-page summary for all-hands communication
5. Establish quarterly strategy review cadence

**Quarterly Rhythm:**
- Week 1: Reflect on past quarter performance against KRs
- Week 2-3: Develop/refine next quarter OKRs based on learnings
- Week 4: Leadership alignment and finalization
- Ongoing: Monthly progress reviews, weekly executive syncs
